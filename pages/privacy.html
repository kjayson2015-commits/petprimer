Privacy

Draft — to be finalized before launch.

Pet Primer collects only what it needs to run the site: your email address when you create an account or join the membership waitlist, the display name you choose, and the posts, replies and photos you share on the community board.

Your email is used to sign you in and, if you ask, to tell you when memberships open. It is never sold. Posts and photos you share on the community board are public. You can ask for your account and posts to be deleted at any time.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and stores account and community data with Supabase.

Questions: contact Pet Primer through Instagram or X.
