Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Pet Primer (petprimer.online) is run by Gilbert Jones. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we collect as little as possible, we never sell it, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in, your email address and the display name you choose. Your email is used to sign you in and is never shown publicly.
  • What you post. Community posts, replies, photos, likes, follows and reports you send, plus anything you add to your profile (a bio, and your pet’s name, type, breed, birth year and photo). These are public on the site, except reports.
  • Age confirmation. When you sign in you confirm you are 16 or older.
  • Membership waitlist. If you join the waitlist, your email address, so we can tell you when memberships open.
  • Visit counts. When a page loads, the site records which page was viewed and the website you came from (for example google.com). It does not record your name, email, IP address or device, does not use cookies for this, and does not follow you across other websites. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal, no visit is recorded.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your information.
  • We don’t use advertising trackers or share data with advertisers.
  • We don’t send marketing emails unless you joined the waitlist or asked for them.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores accounts, community posts, photos, the waitlist and visit counts.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files. Like any web host, it may keep basic technical logs.
  • Email delivery for sign-in links is handled by our email provider.

Your browser stores a small sign-in token so you stay signed in. Clearing your browser data or signing out removes it.

Your choices

  • You can delete your own community posts at any time.
  • You can ask for your account, posts, photos and waitlist entry to be deleted, or for a copy of what we hold about you, by emailing petprimer.care@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Children

Pet Primer is meant for adults. Accounts aren’t intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Important changes will also be posted on the site.

Contact

Email petprimer.care@gmail.com, or message Pet Primer on Instagram or X.
