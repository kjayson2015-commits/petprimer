# Pet Primer community: illegal content risk assessment

Prepared for the UK Online Safety Act 2023 (user-to-user service duties). Owner: Gilbert Jones. Date: 17 October 2026. Review: every 12 months, or before any significant change (for example adding private messages, video, or paid memberships).

## The service

- A public community board at petprimer.online/community where signed-in members post short text and photos about dogs and cats, reply, like and follow.
- No private messaging, no live streaming, no video, no groups, no adult content.
- Accounts are for people aged 16 and over (confirmed at sign-in; stated in the terms).
- Expected users: dog and cat owners, mostly in the US and UK. Small service, run by one person.

## Risks considered

| Kind of illegal harm | Likelihood here | Why | Main measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Animal cruelty content | Low to medium | The topic is animals, so it is the most relevant risk | Rule against content that shows or encourages harm; report button; auto-hide at 3 reports; editor review |
| Fraud and scams (e.g. pet-selling scams) | Low to medium | Pet sales scams are common online | Rule against selling animals and advertising; link labelling (nofollow, ugc); reports |
| Harassment and threats | Low | Public, topic-focused board, no private messages | Kindness rule; reports; account suspension |
| Hate content | Low | Not linked to the topic | Rules; reports; removal |
| Child sexual abuse material and grooming | Low | Photo uploads exist, but no private messaging, no search of users by age, 16+ accounts, public-only conversations | No DMs; photos are public and reportable; immediate removal and reporting to the authorities if ever found |
| Terrorism, drugs, weapons, self-harm | Low | Unrelated to the topic | Rules; reports; removal |
| Dangerous animal-health advice (not illegal, but harmful) | Medium | Members may give confident wrong advice | Notice that replies are opinions; emergency page linked from the board; editor can remove |

Overall risk level: low.

## Measures in place

1. Community rules in the terms, linked from the board.
2. Report button on every post and reply; one report per member per item.
3. Automatic hiding of any post or reply reported by three different members, until reviewed.
4. Editor moderation screen with reports and removal.
5. Accounts can be suspended.
6. No private messages, so all interaction is public.
7. 16+ age confirmation at sign-in.
8. A contact address for reports and complaints: petprimer.care@gmail.com.

## Records

Reports and moderation actions are kept in the database (the reports table with status open, kept, warned or removed).
